Charity: Water

Data-driven digital marketing strategy designed to accelerate the fight against the global water crisis.

The Mission

The project brief was to create a landing page and social media campaign aimed at persuading college demographics to become regular donors of Charity: Water.

Agency Simulation

Operating under a mock agency I created a mock company named "War on Thirst" and led a mock college student targeting campaign from conception to execution under a collaboration effort between "War on Thirst" and "Charity: Water". This involved persona development, keyword research, and designing visual assets that align with global non-profit standards.
